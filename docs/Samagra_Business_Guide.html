1Samagra in one minute

What is Samagra?

In shortOne portal for sub-€50k sourcing at VW GDS India. Requesters collect supplier quotes and raise complete requests; buyers get them already read and compared, with a market price to negotiate with; management sees turnaround and workload live.

"Samagra" means complete, whole: the whole small-purchase cycle in one place, from the supplier enquiry to the PO number.

One-liner: "High-volume, low-value buying costs Procurement more effort per rupee than anything else it does, and today it runs on email and Excel. Samagra gives that effort back to the buyers."

Terms
Sourcing
Finding and choosing a supplier for something the company needs to buy.
Sub-€50k
Purchases below about €50,000, where the requester collects quotes themselves before Procurement takes over.
PO
Purchase order: the official order to a supplier, with its own number.

What is the single biggest benefit?

In shortBuyers spend their time negotiating instead of chasing documents and rebuilding spreadsheets, and they negotiate with a reference price in hand.

Negotiation is already the longest part of a buyer's work (Himanshu). Samagra helps twice: it frees time for it, and the market price check shows when a supplier sits above what Amazon or Flipkart charge for the same model.

In shortRequests arrive incomplete and bounce by email; quotes come in every format and are rebuilt by hand into one comparison; status means asking someone; and there's no common record of why a supplier won.

3The process, end to end

How does a sub-€50k purchase flow through Samagra?

In shortThe requester floats the enquiry and collects quotes, then raises a complete sourcing request. The case goes to the commodity's buyer, who checks it, compares the offers, checks market prices, negotiates and releases the PO. Every step is recorded.
  1. Enquiry (requester): attach the RFQ, and the AI drafts the supplier note; answer the InfoSec screening; send. Each supplier gets a private link.
  2. Quotes (suppliers): quote, revise (v1, v2, v3…) or decline until the deadline. Samagra compares the latest versions automatically.
  3. Sourcing request (requester): attach cart, SFO, RFQ, offers and, if needed, InfoSec clearance. Incomplete requests can't be submitted.
  4. Routing: the case goes straight to the buyer who owns that commodity.
  5. Check (buyer): confirm the documents; the TAT clock starts from the day the set was complete.
  6. Compare and benchmark (buyer): offers ranked net of tax in one currency; market price from Amazon and Flipkart.
  7. Ask (buyer ↔ requester): questions and answers on the case; small corrections recorded old → new.
  8. Award (buyer): approve with a PO number, which can't be reused on another case.
Terms
RFQ
Request for quotation: the document telling suppliers what to quote for.
Shopping cart (SC)
The approved purchase request raised in SAP before sourcing.
SFO / deviation note
Suggestion for Order: the signed note recommending a supplier, listing the offers.
Commodity
The category of what is bought (e.g. headsets, UPS, AV); decides which buyer handles it.
InfoSec clearance
Information Security's approval, needed when a supplier will touch VW data, systems or networks.

What about frame contracts and requests that arrive by email?

In shortFrame contracts have no cart yet, so they need SFO, RFQ and offers, get an FC- number and close with a contract number. Emailed requests are read from the GP mailbox and proposed as a case for a buyer to confirm.
Terms
Frame contract
An agreement on rates and terms for future orders (call-offs), each raised later.

4What changes for each person

Requester

In shortKnows exactly what's needed, can't submit something incomplete, runs supplier enquiries in one place, sees status without asking, and answers questions on the case itself.

GP buyer

In shortReceives complete cases already routed to them, with offers read and ranked, a market price to negotiate against, and the next action spelled out. Time moves from clerical work to negotiation.

GP head

In shortSees TAT, workload per buyer, spend per department and single sourcing as live numbers with Excel export, plus evidence for every award decision.
Terms
Single sourcing
Buying from one supplier without competing quotes; needs justification and is watched.

Supplier

In shortNeeds only email and a browser: no account, no password. One link to download the RFQ and quote, revise or decline, even from a phone.

InfoSec

In shortEvery request is screened against their five criteria, and nothing that needs clearance reaches Procurement without it.

Does it replace buyers?

In shortNo. It removes the clerical part: checking completeness, reading quotes, building comparison sheets. Every decision stays with a named person.

5Business value and ROI

Where does the value come from?

In shortFour places: time (buyers stop rebuilding comparisons), money (a reference price for negotiation and like-for-like comparison), control (InfoSec enforced, PO uniqueness, full audit), and visibility (live numbers instead of hand-built reports).

What is the ROI?

In shortIf Samagra saves 30–50% of the 600–900 buyer hours, that's 180–450 hours a year. If better negotiation improves price by 1% on ₹35–45 crore, that's ₹35–45 lakh a year. Both are scenarios until a pilot measures them.

Careful: always say "if" and "a pilot would measure it". The price lever applies only to catalogue products, so the real figure depends on that share of spend.
Terms
ROI
Return on investment: what you gain compared with what it costs.
Catalogue product
A standard model sold in shops (a laptop, a UPS), as opposed to a service.

How does GP benchmark prices today, and what does Samagra add?

In shortToday buyers check VW's own purchase history for the same item. Samagra adds what history can't show: what the same model sells for online today. History tells you what you paid; the market tells you what it's worth now.

Purchase history is VW data and stays inside VW. Running inside VW, Samagra would build that history itself from every case (items, every supplier's price, the winner, the date), and past POs could be imported once to start with years of history. That price-history view is on the roadmap, not built.

Terms
Benchmarking
Comparing a price against a reference: past prices or the market.

How would we measure success after six months?

In shortBuyer minutes per case, requests sent back for missing documents, share of cases within TAT, savings negotiated with the market check, and user feedback, each against the period before.

Will it handle more users and more data?

In shortYes. Ten years of GP India is about 100 MB of data, which is small. For VW-wide use the business rules stay and the infrastructure grows: a database server, cloud file storage, several app copies, VW login. The Technical Guide lists every change.

What is already configurable without code: departments, commodities and which buyer owns them, TAT bands, InfoSec questions, document types. A new entity is mostly configuration plus an "entity" field so each sees only its own cases.

What does it take to run, and what does it cost?

In shortNo licence fees: the software is open source. Running costs are a small VW cloud setup, AI usage (low, because each document is read once) and a price-search plan. Exact figures come after VW IT quotes the platform.
Careful: don't give a rupee figure for running costs before IT has quoted. Compare it with 600–900 buyer hours a year.
Terms
Open source
Software whose code is public and free to use (Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL).

How do we get people to use it?

In shortIt's less work for each of them from day one: requesters get status without chasing, suppliers need only a link, buyers stop rebuilding spreadsheets. Training is minimal: a guided form and a case page that says what to do next.

8Data, compliance and control

What data does Samagra hold, and who sees it?

In shortCases, their documents, supplier quotes and the audit trail; personal data is limited to names, work emails and roles. Requesters see their own cases, buyers the queue, suppliers one page each.

Does VW data leave VW?

In shortNot in production. AI requests go to VW's own LLMaaS; mail goes through Microsoft, which VW already uses; only the product name for the price check (e.g. "APC BX1100C-IN") goes to the search service, never prices or suppliers.

How does it support audits and compliance?

In shortEvery action is recorded with who and when; every supplier version and every correction (old → new) is kept; InfoSec clearance is enforced; a PO number can't be on two cases. "Why did supplier X win?" is answerable months later.

Before production it goes through VW's InfoSec and data-protection review, with a retention period for closed cases.

Terms
TISAX / ISO 27001
Information-security certifications suppliers may be asked for when they handle VW information.
NDA
Non-disclosure agreement.
Retention period
How long records must be kept by law and policy.

11Tough questions

Why build this instead of buying SAP Ariba or Coupa?

In shortThose suites cover the whole procure-to-pay process, with licences and long rollouts. Samagra targets one narrow, high-volume flow with VW's own forms and rules. Its core value, AI reading and fair comparison, could also sit beside a suite if VW already uses one.
Careful: don't claim VW has no such tool. Say you would check with GP.
Terms
Procure-to-pay
The whole chain from purchase request to paying the invoice.

Another team solved the same problem. Why yours?

In shortDon't compare; show what's specific: built on GP's own figures, the supplier enquiry with versions and a link that works on a phone, the market price check, InfoSec enforcement, every AI figure verified, and an honest list of what's simulated.

What if suppliers' online prices are always lower?

In shortThat's the point: it's a negotiation lever, not a rule. Retail prices often lack a GST invoice to VW, enterprise warranty, delivery to site or set-up; the buyer weighs those and asks the supplier to explain the gap.

How long until it's in production?

In shortThat depends on VW IT's hosting and the InfoSec review, so don't name a date. The pilot can start on a single VW server as soon as those are approved.
